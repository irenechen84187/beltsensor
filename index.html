Research Analysis Thresholds

Adjust mathematical criteria for detecting breathing stability and irregularities for research analysis. These parameters produce measurable evidence without making clinical treatment decisions.

Max Duration Variability (CV_dur %)
Cycles with CV_dur > threshold are flagged as irregular rhythm (default: 15.0%)
Max Amplitude Variability (CV_amp %)
Cycles with CV_amp > threshold are flagged as irregular amplitude (default: 20.0%)
Breathing Pause Limit (seconds)
Time without inhalation peak before flagging apnea / breath hold (default: 8.0s)
Unusual Amplitude Jump Ratio
Breath excursion > ratio × mean excursion flagged as sigh / jump (default: 1.70x)
Baseline Drift Limit (N)
Transducer baseline shift from tare zero before drift alert (default: 1.50 N)
Sensor Noise Ripple Limit (N)
High frequency ripple above limit marks poor signal quality separately (default: 0.20 N)
Min Peak Prominence (N)
Minimum excursion above trough to register a breath peak (default: 0.35 N)
